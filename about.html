Our Story

About Ember & Quill

Ember & Quill started with a simple belief: Nigerian food, cooked over real fire, is among the best in the world. We set out to create a space where that fire meets contemporary technique — where the smoky depth of suya and grilled meats sits comfortably alongside refined plating and modern flavour combinations.

Every dish on our menu tells a story. From the pepper snail recipes passed down through generations to our take on the classic ribeye, each plate is a conversation between where we've been and where we're going.

What Makes Us Different

Fire-cooked. Fresh. Intentional.

Fire-Cooked

Everything touches the flame. Our charcoal grill and wood-fired oven give every dish a smoky depth you can't get from a conventional kitchen.

Fresh Ingredients

We source daily from local markets in Abuja. Our vegetables, meats, and seafood are never frozen — they arrive fresh and go straight to the fire.

Intentional Craft

Every spice blend, every sauce, every garnish is made in-house. We don't cut corners — we build flavour from scratch, every single day.
