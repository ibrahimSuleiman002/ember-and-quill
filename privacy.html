Privacy Policy

Last updated: · Applies to Ember & Quill, Abuja, Nigeria.

1. Introduction

Ember & Quill respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, how long we keep it, and how you can ask us to correct or delete it. It is intended to follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

2. What data we collect

When you interact with us, we may collect the following personal data:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Delivery addresses (for orders delivered to you)
  • Reservation details (date, time, number of guests)
  • Order details (what you ordered, any special requests)
  • Messages you send us through WhatsApp or by phone

3. Why we collect it

We collect this information to take your reservations and orders, and to contact you about them. This includes confirming a table, confirming an order, letting you know when a delivery is on its way, and responding to any questions or special requests you send us.

4. Who can see your data

Only the Ember & Quill team who need your information to take your reservation or order can see it. We do not sell your personal data to anyone, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing. If we ever work with a service provider (for example, a delivery partner) who needs your details to complete your order, we only share the minimum information required to do that job.

5. Payments

Payment card details are never stored on our own systems. If you pay by card, the payment is handled entirely by a secure, licensed payment provider. We only ever see the outcome of the payment (whether it succeeded), never your full card number or security code.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your personal data only for as long as it is needed to fulfil your reservation or order, and for a short period afterwards so we can deal with any follow-up questions. Reservation and order records are reviewed regularly and deleted when they are no longer needed.

7. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Show you the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct any data that is wrong or out of date
  • Delete your personal data
  • Stop us from using your data for a particular purpose

8. How to contact us

To ask for your data to be corrected or deleted, or to ask any question about this policy, call us on 07063134521. We will respond to your request as soon as we can.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Please check back occasionally to stay informed.
