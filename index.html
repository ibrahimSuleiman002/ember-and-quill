Our Story

Born from fire, crafted with intention

Ember & Quill is a modern Nigerian restaurant where open-fire cooking meets contemporary technique. Every dish is a conversation between smoke and spice, tradition and innovation.

From the charcoal grill to the hand-written menu that changes with the seasons, every detail invites you to slow down and savour.
